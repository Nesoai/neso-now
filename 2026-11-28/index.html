How a TIF district works

Next, a lesson. Tax increment financing, or TIF, uses taxes on future gains in real estate values to pay for new improvements.

It begins when a geographic area is designated as a TIF district. A TIF district usually runs for 20 to 25 years.

During that time, the real estate tax revenue above the base at the time the district was set up flows into the TIF, where it can repay bonds or pay for projects as they go. TIF is authorized by state law in nearly all 50 states.

In many states an area must be blighted before a TIF district can be set up, so the money goes where development might not otherwise happen.

How the housing tax credit works

Next, a lesson. The Low-Income Housing Tax Credit was created by the Tax Reform Act of 1986.

What a 1031 exchange is

Here is a short lesson. In a like-kind exchange, real property used for business or held as an investment is exchanged for other business or investment property of the same type.

Generally, no gain or loss has to be recognized, under Section 1031 of the Internal Revenue Code. If money or other property that is not like-kind is also received in the exchange, a gain must be recognized to the extent of that money and property.

Since January 1, 2018, Section 1031 applies only to exchanges of real property. Real properties are generally like-kind whether they are improved or unimproved, but real property in the United States is not like-kind to real property outside it.
