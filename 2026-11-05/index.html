What the federal funds rate is

Here is a short lesson. The federal funds rate is the interest rate charged by banks to borrow from each other overnight.

The Federal Reserve sets a target range for it. Changes to that target range influence short-term interest rates for other financial instruments, which in turn affect the spending decisions of households and businesses.

What a data center is

A short lesson, in plain words. A data center is a building filled with computer servers, along with the power and cooling equipment that keeps them running. It is where websites, online services and stored files physically live.

The Department of Energy calls data centers one of the most energy-intensive building types. They can use 10 to 50 times the energy per floor space of a typical commercial office building.

The Department of Energy reported that data centers used about 4.4 percent of all U.S. electricity in 2023. Its report projected they could use about 6.7 to 12 percent by 2028.

What a brownfield is

A short lesson, in plain words. Federal law describes a brownfield as real property whose expansion, redevelopment or reuse may be complicated by the presence, or potential presence, of a hazardous substance, pollutant or contaminant.

Common contaminants at these sites include lead, asbestos and petroleum. Addressing the real or perceived threat of contamination prevents or limits harmful exposure.

The Environmental Protection Agency's brownfields program has converted once neglected sites to boost local economies and enhance quality of life for nearby residents.
