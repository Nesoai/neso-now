How CDFIs and SBA loans work

Here is a short lesson. Community Development Financial Institutions, or CDFIs, are specialized organizations that provide financial services in low-income communities and to people who lack access to financing.

They include community development banks and credit unions. A 7(a) loan is the Small Business Administration's main loan program for small businesses.

Lenders make the loans and the SBA provides a loan guarantee to them; it does not lend to borrowers directly.

What a data center is

Here is a short lesson. A data center is a building filled with computer servers, along with the power and cooling equipment that keeps them running. It is where websites, online services and stored files physically live.

The Department of Energy calls data centers one of the most energy-intensive building types. They can use 10 to 50 times the energy per floor space of a typical commercial office building.

The Department of Energy reported that data centers used about 4.4 percent of all U.S. electricity in 2023. Its report projected they could use about 6.7 to 12 percent by 2028.

For a data center or any large load, the interconnection queue — the line to connect to the grid — is usually the binding constraint, more often than land or zoning.

What the federal funds rate is

Next, a lesson. The federal funds rate is the interest rate charged by banks to borrow from each other overnight.

The Federal Reserve sets a target range for it. Changes to that target range influence short-term interest rates for other financial instruments, which in turn affect the spending decisions of households and businesses.

What Opportunity Zones are

A short lesson, in plain words. Opportunity Zones are an economic development tool that allows people to invest in distressed areas in the United States.

Their purpose is to spur economic growth and job creation in low-income communities. Investors in them can receive tax benefits.

The capital stack and DSCR

Here is a short lesson. A capital stack is every source of money in a project, from senior debt to equity, in the order each gets repaid.

When the sources do not add up to the cost, the shortfall is called the gap. DSCR, the debt service coverage ratio, divides a building's net operating income by its loan payments.

Lenders use it to ask whether the building can carry its debt.

What a brownfield is

A short lesson, in plain words. Federal law describes a brownfield as real property whose expansion, redevelopment or reuse may be complicated by the presence, or potential presence, of a hazardous substance, pollutant or contaminant.

Common contaminants at these sites include lead, asbestos and petroleum. Addressing the real or perceived threat of contamination prevents or limits harmful exposure.

The Environmental Protection Agency's brownfields program has converted once neglected sites to boost local economies and enhance quality of life for nearby residents.
